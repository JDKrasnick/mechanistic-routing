Signal-vs-noise audit (on collected data)

The existing records are healthy — emotion measures 0.52 vs 0.167 chance, so the target signal is real, not sampling jitter. Two noise-limited regions to manage as collection scales:

  • Utility spread is 6.5× the noise floor (std 0.162 vs 1-label = 0.025) — the variation across candidates is genuine signal.
  • ~43% of candidate effects move ≤2 labels, i.e. sit near the floor. Ranking within this near-zero group is noise-limited and caps pairwise/Spearman accuracy.
  • Retention is the noisiest component — 54% of retention deltas are within one label (N=24 eval batch). It feeds the utility penalty, so it injects noise into the target.
  • Mitigation is already in the data: margin_improvement correlates 0.79 with accuracy and is lower-variance — regress the critic on it, not raw acc.
  • 68% of candidates are net-harmful — much of the critic's value is declining damage, consistent with the M1 finding.
